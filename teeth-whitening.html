About teeth whitening

Teeth whitening lightens the natural colour of teeth under a dentist's supervision. At Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East, whitening starts with a check-up to make sure teeth and gums are healthy.

Whitening can help with

  • Yellowing from age
  • Stains from tea, coffee or tobacco
  • A dull overall shade

Teeth whitening: questions

Is teeth whitening safe?

Whitening done or supervised by a dentist is considered safe for healthy teeth and gums.

How long do whitening results last?

Results commonly last from several months to about 2 years, depending on diet and habits like tea, coffee and smoking.

Does whitening cause sensitivity?

Some people feel temporary sensitivity for a day or two. It usually settles on its own.

Will whitening work on crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening changes natural teeth only, so existing crowns and fillings may need replacing to match.

Who should not get teeth whitening?

It is usually postponed during pregnancy and breastfeeding, and until cavities or gum problems are treated.

What should I avoid after whitening?

For the first 48 hours avoid tea, coffee, cola, turmeric-heavy food and smoking.

Does scaling whiten teeth?

Scaling removes surface stains and tartar but does not change the tooth's natural shade.
