About clear aligners

Clear aligners are thin, removable plastic trays that move teeth gradually into place. Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East offers clear aligner treatment for teens and adults.

Aligners can help with

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Mild to moderate bite problems
  • Teeth that moved back after earlier braces

What to expect

  1. Consultation and examination
  2. Digital scan or impressions and X-rays
  3. Treatment plan showing expected tooth movement
  4. New set of aligners every 1 to 2 weeks
  5. Regular check-ups, then retainers to hold the result

Clear aligners: frequently asked questions

What are clear aligners?

They are custom-made transparent trays that fit over your teeth and move them a little at a time. You switch to a new set every 1 to 2 weeks.

Am I suitable for clear aligners?

Aligners work well for many cases of crowding, spacing and mild to moderate bite problems. Some complex cases are better treated with braces. An examination will tell you which suits you.

How long does aligner treatment take?

Most cases take about 6 to 18 months, depending on how much the teeth need to move.

How many hours a day do I wear aligners?

About 20 to 22 hours a day. You remove them only to eat, drink anything other than water, and brush.

Do clear aligners hurt?

You may feel pressure or tightness for a day or two with each new set. This means the teeth are moving and it settles quickly.

Are aligners visible?

They are nearly invisible when worn, which is why many adults prefer them to braces.

Clear aligners or braces: which is better?

Aligners are removable and less visible but need discipline to wear all day. Braces stay fixed and suit more complex movements. The dentist will explain which fits your case.

How much do clear aligners cost in Jogeshwari East?

Cost depends on how complex your case is and how many aligner sets you need. You get an estimate after the scan and treatment plan.

Do I need a retainer after aligners?

Yes. Retainers stop teeth from drifting back. Wearing them as advised protects your result.

Can adults get clear aligners?

Yes. There is no upper age limit as long as your gums and bone are healthy.

Can I eat with aligners in?

No. Take them out to eat and brush before putting them back, so food is not trapped against your teeth.
