Reel studio (clinic staff)

Insert the patient photos and make a reel for Instagram, WhatsApp or Google. This page is not linked from the website.

Before posting: use a patient’s face only with their written consent. Label AI-generated content as AI, and do not present a simulation as a real treatment result.

1. Reveal reels (free, made on this device)

Pick a style, then make an 8 second 9:16 reel. Needs both photos, front-facing, similar distance.

2. Photo tools (free): crop, rotate, watermark

Straighten and crop any photo to reel, post or square shape, add the clinic watermark, and download it.

3. AI smiling clip (costs about ₹40 to ₹60 each)

Animates the photo (the after photo, or the before photo if that is the only one) into a short video. Clinic staff numbers only. Limited to 10 clips a day.

4. Post queue (clinic staff): send to Google as a draft

Saves the image and caption as a draft. Nothing is posted from here. The doctor reviews each draft and approves it before it goes on Google. Google posts take a photo, not a video, so a reel is sent as its final frame.

5. Daily education reel (free, from the 365-day plan)

Makes a 12 second 9:16 reel for the chosen day: the 3D animation (or 3D picture) with the day’s title, points and clinic details. Days with a 3D clip are marked 🎬. Today’s day is picked for you. No patient photos are needed.

6. Procedure stories (3D, no text)

Step-by-step 3D sequence that explains the treatment on its own: implant, All-on-4 upper and lower, overdenture, root canal. Every step was checked for dental accuracy before use.
