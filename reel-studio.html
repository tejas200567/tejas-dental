Reel studio (clinic staff)

Insert the patient photos and make a reel for Instagram, WhatsApp or Google. This page is not linked from the website.

Before posting: use a patient’s face only with their written consent. Label AI-generated content as AI, and do not present a simulation as a real treatment result.

1. Mesh-scan reveal reel (free, made on this device)

A 3D face mesh scans down the face and reveals the new smile. 8 seconds, 9:16. Needs both photos, front-facing, similar distance.

2. AI smiling clip (costs about ₹40 to ₹60 each)

Animates the photo (the after photo, or the before photo if that is the only one) into a short video. Clinic staff numbers only. Limited to 10 clips a day.
