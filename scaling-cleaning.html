Scaling and teeth cleaning: frequently asked questions

What is dental scaling?

Scaling is professional removal of hardened plaque, called tartar or calculus, from the teeth and under the gum line.

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

Most people benefit from a cleaning every 6 to 12 months. People with gum disease may need it more often.

Does scaling damage tooth enamel?

No. Done correctly, scaling removes deposits without harming enamel.

Why are my teeth sensitive after scaling?

Tartar can cover parts of the tooth. Once it is removed, mild sensitivity for a few days is common and usually settles.

What do bleeding gums mean?

Bleeding while brushing is often an early sign of gum inflammation. A cleaning and better home care usually help.

How long does a scaling appointment take?

Usually about 30 to 45 minutes, depending on how much tartar there is.

Does cleaning make teeth whiter?

It removes surface stains, so teeth often look brighter, but it does not change the natural shade. Whitening is a separate treatment.

Is scaling painful?

Most people feel only vibration and water spray. Sensitive areas can be numbed if needed.
