About tooth extraction and wisdom teeth

Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East carries out routine and surgical tooth extractions, including wisdom teeth, under local anaesthesia after an X-ray.

A tooth may need removal when

  • It is too broken or decayed to repair
  • A wisdom tooth is stuck, infected or causing pain
  • Severe gum disease has loosened it
  • Space is needed for orthodontic treatment

What to expect

  1. Examination and X-ray
  2. Local anaesthesia
  3. Removal of the tooth
  4. Gauze to control bleeding
  5. Aftercare instructions and follow-up if needed

Tooth extraction and wisdom teeth: questions

Is tooth extraction painful?

The area is numbed with local anaesthesia, so you feel pressure but not pain. Some soreness afterwards is normal for a few days.

Does every wisdom tooth need to be removed?

No. Wisdom teeth that come in straight and can be cleaned are often left alone. Removal is advised if they cause pain, infection or damage.

What are signs of a problem wisdom tooth?

Pain at the back of the jaw, swollen gums, bad taste, difficulty opening the mouth or food getting stuck behind the last tooth.

What should I do after an extraction?

Bite on the gauze for 30 to 45 minutes. For 24 hours avoid spitting, rinsing hard, straws, smoking and hot food. Eat soft, cool food.

How long does it take to heal after extraction?

The gum usually closes in 1 to 2 weeks. The bone underneath takes a few months to fully heal.

What is a dry socket?

It is a painful condition when the blood clot comes out of the socket, usually 2 to 4 days after removal. Contact the clinic if pain gets worse instead of better.

Can I go to work after an extraction?

Most people return to work the next day after a routine extraction. A surgical wisdom tooth removal may need a day or two of rest.

Should I replace an extracted tooth?

Replacing a missing back or front tooth with an implant, bridge or denture helps prevent neighbouring teeth from shifting.

Do you take an X-ray before extraction?

Yes. An X-ray shows the root shape and nearby structures so the extraction can be planned safely.
