About dental implants

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root. A crown is fixed on top so it looks and works like a natural tooth. Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East plans and provides implant treatment.

Implants are an option if you have

  • One or more missing teeth
  • A tooth that must be removed
  • Loose dentures you want to stabilise

What to expect

  1. Examination and X-rays to check the bone
  2. Implant placed under local anaesthesia
  3. Healing period while the implant bonds with bone
  4. Crown fixed on the implant
  5. Regular check-ups and cleaning

Dental implants: frequently asked questions

What is a dental implant?

It is an artificial tooth root, usually titanium, placed in the jawbone. A crown, bridge or denture is attached to it.

Am I suitable for dental implants?

You need healthy gums and enough jawbone. Smoking and uncontrolled diabetes raise the risk of failure. An examination and X-ray will confirm suitability.

Is getting an implant painful?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia. Most people have mild soreness and swelling for a few days afterwards.

How long does implant treatment take?

Often about 3 to 6 months from placement to the final crown, because the implant needs time to bond with the bone.

How long do dental implants last?

With good brushing, cleaning and regular check-ups, implants can last many years, often for life. The crown on top may need replacing over time.

How much does a dental implant cost in Jogeshwari East?

Cost depends on the implant system, whether bone grafting is needed and the type of crown. You get an estimate after the examination.

Implant, bridge or denture: which is better?

An implant does not need neighbouring teeth to be trimmed, unlike a bridge, and is more stable than a removable denture. The right choice depends on your mouth and budget.

What if I don't have enough bone?

A bone graft may be needed first. The dentist will explain this after checking the X-ray.

Can older people get implants?

Yes. Age alone is not a barrier if general health, gums and bone are suitable.

Can implants hold dentures in place?

Yes. Implant-supported dentures clip onto implants, so they don't slip while eating or speaking.
