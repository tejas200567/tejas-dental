The camera guides each pose with instructions on screen and by voice, then counts 3, 2, 1 and takes the photo by itself. With the back camera the dentist holds the phone and the patient follows the voice. Even light, phone at face height, about 40 cm away.

Guide lines: blue = pupil line, yellow = facial midline (perpendicular through the centre of the pupils), pink = mouth-corner line. The face is built from the 2D photos, so depth is approximate. It is a planning and communication aid, not a measured 3D scan.

Complete the scan first.

The millimetre values use an average adult pupil distance of 63 mm as the scale, so they are approximate (about ±2–3 mm). Confirm clinically before treatment decisions.

Save the scan as one file on this phone to compare visits later. The file holds the patient's photos: keep it private.
