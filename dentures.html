About dentures

Dentures are removable replacements for missing teeth. Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East makes complete and partial dentures, and fits and adjusts them in the clinic.

Dentures suit people who

  • Have lost all teeth in one or both jaws
  • Have several missing teeth
  • Want a non-surgical tooth replacement

  2. Bite records and tooth shade selection
  3. Trial fitting
  4. Final denture fitting
  5. Adjustment visits as needed

Dentures: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between complete and partial dentures?

Complete dentures replace all teeth in a jaw. Partial dentures replace some teeth and clip onto the remaining natural teeth.

How long does it take to make dentures?

Usually several visits over about 2 to 3 weeks.

How long does it take to get used to dentures?

Most people adjust to speaking and eating within a few weeks. Sore spots can be adjusted at the clinic.

Can I eat normally with dentures?

Start with soft food cut into small pieces and chew on both sides. Most foods become manageable with practice.

Should I sleep with my dentures?

It is usually advised to remove them at night to rest the gums, and keep them in water.

How do I clean dentures?

Brush them daily with a soft brush and denture cleaner, not regular toothpaste, and rinse after meals.

How long do dentures last?

Often 5 to 10 years. The gums change shape over time, so dentures may need relining or remaking.

What if my dentures are loose?

Visit the clinic. A reline can improve the fit, and implant-supported dentures are an option for more stability.
