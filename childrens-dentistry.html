Children's dentistry: frequently asked questions

When should my child first see a dentist?

By their first birthday or when the first tooth appears.

Do milk teeth with cavities need treatment?

Yes. Untreated cavities in milk teeth can cause pain and infection and affect the permanent teeth growing underneath.

How often should children visit the dentist?

Every 6 months is a common recommendation.

How can I prevent cavities in my child?

Brush twice a day with an age-appropriate amount of fluoride toothpaste, limit sweets and sugary drinks between meals, and avoid bedtime milk bottles.

Is thumb sucking harmful?

It is normal in young children but can affect tooth position if it continues after permanent teeth start coming in.

When should my child be checked for braces?

An orthodontic check around age 7 helps spot problems early, even if braces start later.
