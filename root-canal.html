About root canal treatment

Root canal treatment removes infected or inflamed pulp from inside a tooth, cleans and seals the canals, and saves the tooth from extraction. At Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East, every root canal is planned with a digital RVG X-ray.

Signs you may need a root canal

  • Pain that lingers after hot or cold food or drinks
  • Pain when biting or chewing
  • Swelling or a pimple-like bump on the gum
  • A tooth that has darkened
  • A deep cavity or a cracked tooth

What to expect

  1. Examination and digital X-ray
  2. Local anaesthesia to numb the tooth
  3. Cleaning and shaping of the root canals
  4. Filling and sealing the canals
  5. A crown, usually advised for back teeth

Root canal treatment: frequently asked questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

It is done under local anaesthesia, so most patients find it feels similar to getting a filling. Mild soreness for a few days afterwards is common and settles with the painkillers the dentist advises.

How many sittings does a root canal take?

Usually 1 to 3 sittings. It depends on how infected the tooth is, how many canals it has and how it responds between visits.

How much does a root canal cost in Jogeshwari East?

The cost depends on the tooth, the number of canals and whether a crown is needed. You get a clear estimate after the examination and X-ray, before treatment starts.

Do I need a crown after a root canal?

Back teeth usually need a crown because a treated tooth becomes more brittle. Front teeth sometimes need only a filling. The dentist will advise after treatment.

How long does a root-canal-treated tooth last?

With a good seal, a crown where needed and regular check-ups, a treated tooth can last many years, often for life.

Can I eat after root canal treatment?

Wait until the numbness wears off. Avoid chewing hard food on that side until the permanent filling or crown is placed.

What happens if I delay a root canal?

The infection can spread to the bone and cause an abscess, swelling and more pain. The tooth may become impossible to save.

Is it better to remove the tooth instead?

Keeping your natural tooth is usually the better choice. Removing it leaves a gap that may later need a bridge or implant.

Do you use digital X-rays for root canals?

Yes. The clinic uses digital RVG X-rays, which give an instant image with lower radiation than film X-rays.

Can a root canal be done in one sitting?

Some teeth can be completed in a single sitting when there is no active infection. The dentist decides after examining the tooth.
