About crowns and bridges

A crown is a cap that covers and protects a damaged tooth. A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring false teeth to neighbouring teeth. Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East provides ceramic and metal-ceramic crowns and bridges.

Crowns and bridges are used for

  • Teeth after root canal treatment
  • Badly broken or heavily filled teeth
  • Cracked teeth
  • Replacing a missing tooth between two healthy teeth

What to expect

  1. Examination and X-ray
  2. Shaping the tooth or teeth
  3. Impression or scan and a temporary crown
  4. Fitting and cementing the final crown or bridge

Crowns and bridges: frequently asked questions

What is a dental crown?

It is a custom-made cap that covers the whole visible part of a tooth to restore its shape, strength and look.

When do I need a crown?

Common reasons are after a root canal, a large broken filling, a cracked tooth or a badly worn tooth.

What materials are crowns made of?

The clinic offers ceramic and metal-ceramic crowns. The dentist will suggest the best option for the tooth's position and your bite.

How many visits does a crown need?

Usually 2 visits: one to prepare the tooth and take an impression, and one to fit the final crown.

How long do crowns last?

With good care, crowns commonly last 10 years or more.

Bridge or implant: which is better?

A bridge is quicker and needs no surgery, but the neighbouring teeth are trimmed. An implant leaves neighbouring teeth untouched. The dentist will explain the trade-offs.

How do I care for a crown or bridge?

Brush twice a day and clean under the bridge with a floss threader or interdental brush. Avoid biting very hard objects.
