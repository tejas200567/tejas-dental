AI digital smile design

Upload one front-facing smile photo. The AI marks your 8 upper front teeth, compares their proportions with ideal smile dimensions, and draws an ideal-proportion design and smile mock-up on your own photo. You can change the proportion and shade, compare before and after, and download the result.

Please note: the "after" picture is an AI simulation for ideas only. It is not a diagnosis or a guarantee. The real plan is made after an examination, X-rays and a discussion of options like whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns or aligners.

How to take the photo

Drawings for illustration. A good photo gives a much more accurate result.

  1. Ask someone to take the photo, or use the back camera with a timer. A selfie at arm's length is fine too.
  2. Stand facing a window in daylight. Turn off flash, beauty mode, portrait mode and filters.
  3. Hold the phone straight, at the height of your mouth, not above or below.
  4. Look straight into the camera, relax, then give a big natural smile showing your upper teeth.
  5. Check the photo is sharp and your teeth are clearly visible before uploading.

The camera checks your position, smile and light, then takes the photo automatically.

For best results: face the camera straight, phone at mouth level, natural full smile showing your upper teeth, good daylight, no filter.

  • The two central incisors match in size and shape and are the most prominent teeth.
  • Seen from the front, each tooth looks narrower than the one before it: laterals about 62–75% of the centrals, canines about 62–80% of the laterals.
  • The teeth midline lines up with the middle of the face.
  • The edges of the upper teeth follow a gentle curve parallel to the lower lip (the smile arc).
  • The gum line is even: centrals and canines level, laterals slightly lower.
  • A natural shade, not an artificial bright white.

Questions

What is digital smile design?

Digital smile design (DSD) uses photos and measurements of your face and teeth to plan a smile makeover, so you can preview the result before treatment.

Is the AI after picture what I will actually get?

No. It is an AI simulation for ideas only. Your real result depends on your teeth, gums, bite and the treatment chosen after an examination.

What are the ideal dimensions of front teeth?

On average, an upper central incisor is about 8.5–9 mm wide and 10.5–11 mm long, a width-to-height ratio of about 75–80%. Laterals are about 6.5 mm wide and canines about 7.5 mm.

Which treatments can change my smile?

Depending on your teeth: whitening, tooth-coloured bonding, veneers, crowns, aligners or braces, and sometimes gum contouring. The dentist advises after examining you.
