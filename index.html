About the clinic

Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic is a neighbourhood dental practice in Jogeshwari East, Mumbai, led by Dr. Tejas Pradhan (Maharashtra State Dental Council Reg. No. A-20222). Families from Kokan Nagar, Poonam Nagar and Andheri East come here for check-ups, root canals, extractions, crowns, dentures, implants and orthodontic treatment.

The clinic uses digital RVG X-rays and sterilises every instrument before each patient. Treatment plans and costs are explained before any work begins.

Treatments

  • Root canal treatment — Root canal treatment removes infected or inflamed pulp from inside a tooth, cleans and seals the canals, and saves the tooth from extraction.
  • Clear aligners — Clear aligners are thin, removable plastic trays that move teeth gradually into place.
  • Braces — Braces use brackets and wires to straighten teeth and correct the bite.
  • Dental implants — A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root.
  • Tooth extraction and wisdom teeth — Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East carries out routine and surgical tooth extractions, including wisdom teeth, under local anaesthesia after an X-ray.
  • Scaling and teeth cleaning — Scaling and polishing remove tartar, plaque and stains that brushing cannot.
  • Crowns and bridges — A crown is a cap that covers and protects a damaged tooth.
  • Dentures — Dentures are removable replacements for missing teeth.
  • Teeth whitening — Teeth whitening lightens the natural colour of teeth under a dentist's supervision.
  • Tooth-coloured fillings — Tooth-coloured composite fillings repair cavities and small chips and blend with your natural teeth.
  • Children's dentistry — Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East sees children for check-ups, fillings and preventive care, in a calm and friendly setting for families.
