About the clinic

Careful dental care for the whole family

Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic is a family dental practice in Jogeshwari East, Mumbai, led by Dr. Tejas Pradhan (Maharashtra State Dental Council Reg. No. A-20222). Families from Kokan Nagar, Poonam Nagar and Andheri East come here for check-ups, root canals, extractions, crowns, dentures, implants and orthodontic treatment.

The clinic uses digital RVG X-rays and sterilises every instrument before each patient. Treatment plans and costs are explained before any work begins.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic?

Shop No. A-19, Aareshwar Society, opposite Dheeraj Darshan, Kokan Nagar Road, near Kokan Hospital, Jogeshwari East, Mumbai 400060.

What are the clinic timings?

Monday to Saturday, 11 am to 2:30 pm and 5:30 pm to 9 pm. Closed on Sunday.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended. Call or WhatsApp +91 83691 87806 or +91 91675 11565.

Who is the dentist?

Dr. Tejas Pradhan, registered with the Maharashtra State Dental Council, Reg. No. A-20222.

Do you do root canal treatment in Jogeshwari East?

Yes. Root canal treatment is done at the clinic with digital RVG X-rays. The number of sittings depends on the tooth.

Do you offer braces or clear aligners?

Yes. The clinic offers metal braces and clear aligners for teens and adults.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Check-ups, fillings and preventive care for children.

Can I get a wisdom tooth removed here?

Yes. Most wisdom tooth extractions are done in the clinic under local anaesthesia after an X-ray.

Is the clinic accessible, and how can I pay?

The entrance is wheelchair accessible and parking is available. Cash, UPI and card/NFC payments are accepted.
