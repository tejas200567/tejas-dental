About braces

Braces use brackets and wires to straighten teeth and correct the bite. Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East treats children, teens and adults with metal braces.

Braces can correct

  • Crowded or crooked teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Protruding front teeth
  • Overbite, underbite or crossbite

What to expect

  1. Examination, X-rays and photos
  2. Treatment plan and estimate
  3. Fitting the brackets and wires
  4. Adjustment visits every 4 to 6 weeks
  5. Removal of braces and retainers to hold the result

Braces: frequently asked questions

What is the right age for braces?

Many children start between 10 and 14 years, when permanent teeth have come in. An early check around age 7 helps spot problems. Adults can get braces at any age.

How long do braces take?

Usually 12 to 24 months, depending on the case.

Do braces hurt?

Teeth feel sore for a few days after fitting and after each adjustment. Soft food and the painkillers the dentist advises help.

How often do I need to visit during braces?

Adjustment visits are usually every 4 to 6 weeks.

What foods should I avoid with braces?

Avoid hard, sticky and chewy foods such as nuts, toffee, chewing gum and hard sweets, which can break brackets.

How do I clean my teeth with braces?

Brush after every meal, clean around each bracket and use an interdental brush or floss threader. The clinic will show you how.

How much do braces cost in Jogeshwari East?

Cost depends on the type of braces and how long treatment takes. You get an estimate after the examination.

Braces or clear aligners?

Braces are fixed and handle complex movements well. Aligners are removable and less visible. The dentist will advise which suits your teeth.

Do I need a retainer after braces?

Yes. Teeth tend to move back, so a retainer is worn after braces as advised.

What if a bracket breaks?

Call the clinic. A loose bracket is usually not an emergency, but it should be fixed so treatment stays on track.
