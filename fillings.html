About tooth-coloured fillings

Tooth-coloured composite fillings repair cavities and small chips and blend with your natural teeth. They are done at Dr Tejas Pradhan's Dental Clinic in Jogeshwari East, usually in a single visit.

You may need a filling if you have

  • Sensitivity to sweet, hot or cold
  • A visible hole or dark spot
  • Food getting stuck in a tooth
  • A small chip or broken edge

What to expect

  1. Examination and X-ray if needed
  2. Removal of decay
  3. Placement of tooth-coloured material in layers
  4. Hardening with a curing light
  5. Shaping and polishing

Tooth-coloured fillings: frequently asked questions

Are fillings painful?

Small fillings often need no injection. For deeper cavities, local anaesthesia keeps you comfortable.

How long does a filling take?

Usually 20 to 45 minutes per tooth.

Can I eat after a filling?

With composite fillings you can eat once the numbness wears off.

How long do fillings last?

Composite fillings commonly last 5 to 10 years with good care.

What happens if I ignore a cavity?

It can grow into the nerve and need a root canal or extraction instead of a simple filling.

Can old silver fillings be replaced?

Yes, if they are worn, cracked or leaking. The dentist will check whether replacement is needed.
